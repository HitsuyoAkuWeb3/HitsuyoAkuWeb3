# Operating Doctrine

## Purpose

Identity Forensics Lab helps founder-led teams make important work easier to find, approve, protect, hand off, and improve.

## The Three Layers

1. **Private memory:** raw notes, research, and client-sensitive material stay local until they are ready for review.
2. **AI workbench:** people and AI turn selected material into bounded drafts, tasks, checks, and decision packets.
3. **GitHub record:** approved work receives history, ownership, review, and evidence.

The rule is simple: do not push the private vault. Push the approved record of the work.

## Engagement Path

| Stage | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic | Identify the control gap and the first official asset. |
| Reconstruction | Build the approved repositories, rules, and working controls. |
| Governance | Review the system on a recurring cadence and close named gaps. |

## Asset Test

An AI-assisted output becomes a reusable business asset when it has:

- an owner;
- an approval path;
- evidence;
- a reuse path; and
- a lifecycle.
